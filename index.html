Our Journey

Milestones Through Time

1999

Humble Beginnings

Founded as a small motorcycle repair workshop with a vision to serve local riders with honesty and skill.

2005

First Showroom

Opened our first branded showroom and became authorized dealer for Kawasaki and Suzuki.

2010

Multi-Brand Expansion

Added KTM, Yamaha, and Honda to our portfolio. Launched in-house financing program.

2015

Service Excellence

Built state-of-the-art service center with 20 bays. Introduced 24/7 roadside assistance.

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched online booking, virtual showroom, and home delivery service. Expanded to 8 locations.

2024

Regional Leader

12 showrooms, 50,000+ bikes delivered, 98% customer satisfaction. The journey continues...
